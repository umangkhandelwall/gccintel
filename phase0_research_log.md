## COUNT RECONCILIATION TABLE

| Source | Year | GCC Count | Definition Used | Why It Differs |
|--------|------|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| NASSCOM-Zinnov | FY2024 | 1,700+ | Unique parent companies with fully-owned India entities; excludes EOR-only setups and SME GCCs <500 employees | Most conservative; requires "established" entity threshold |
| NASSCOM-Zinnov (centers) | FY2024 | 2,975+ | Physical office locations/units (one company can have 3 offices = 3 units) | Inflated by multi-city presences of large GCCs like Accenture Solutions, HSBC, etc. |
| NASSCOM-Zinnov updated | March 2026 | 2,117 | Corporate entities; definition updated to include GCCs with 50+ employees including EOR-managed | Wider definition + 18 months of new entrants vs. FY2024 |
| NASSCOM-Zinnov units | March 2026 | 3,728 | Physical locations / office sites | Consistent methodology vs. FY2024 units figure |
| Press reports / brief | 2024-26 | "2,000+" | Vague — often citing unit count or projection | Lazy journalism; conflates units with companies, FY2030 projections with actuals |
| State govt portals | Variable | State-specific counts | Approved/registered under state IT/GCC policy; includes companies that got incentives but may not be operational | Double-counts in progress; overestimates operational GCCs |
| JLL/CBRE/Colliers | FY2024 | Not a count — lease volumes | They track leases, not entities | Different data type entirely: RE absorbed by GCCs ≠ GCC count |

**Core insight:** The "right" number depends on the question. For a buyer deciding whether to enter India, the relevant number is operational entities with 50+ employees on a captive basis. That number is ~1,700-1,800 as of FY2024. The 2,000+ figure is either units or projections or a widened definition. **Nobody shows this reconciliation publicly. This is the product's anchor insight.**

## BOLD CALLS REGISTER — PHASE 0

### BOLD CALL #1: The "2,000+ GCCs" figure is a category error — and that is the product's anchor insight.

- **Why it's bold:** Every press release and consultant uses "2,000+" uncritically. Calling this out as wrong/misleading goes against the consensus.
- **Reasoning:** NASSCOM-Zinnov FY2024 clearly states 1,700+ parent entities; the 2,975+ is physical centers/units. The "2,000+" in press uses the widened 2026 definition. A product that shows this reconciliation instantly proves its value.
- **Evidence:** R1, R2, R7 in research log; nasscom.in FY2024 report; astraviseservices.com definitional analysis
- **Alternatives rejected:** Accept the 2,000+ figure and move on. Rejected: this obscures the product's core insight.
- **What would prove me wrong:** If NASSCOM clarifies that 2,000+ is always unique entities with consistent definition since 2022. (They haven't.)
- **Fallback:** If the distinction doesn't resonate, pivot to "the count matters less than the list — and there's no publicly searchable list of which companies those 2,000 are."
- **Confidence:** High — the definitional split between entities and units is documented in the primary NASSCOM-Zinnov report itself.
